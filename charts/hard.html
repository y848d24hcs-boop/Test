ES10 — Early Surrender vs 10

Macau allows surrender before the dealer checks for blackjack, but only when the upcard is a 10-value. The two cells: 15 vs 10 and 16 vs 10.

Crucially: no surrender vs 9, no surrender vs Ace. Standard late-surrender charts you'll see online flag those — ignore them here.

ENHC — No Hole Card

Dealer doesn't peek for blackjack, so you can lose double bets to a dealer natural. The one cell that flips: 11 vs A is Hit, not Double (highlighted in gold).

If your property uses OBO (original-bet-only against dealer BJ), you can revert to standard play and double 11 vs A. Verify at the table.

Composite Edge

With ES10 + S17 + DAS + ENHC, basic strategy yields a house edge near 0.15–0.25% depending on the BJ-bet-loss rule (OBO vs full ENHC). The two ES10 surrender cells alone are worth roughly +0.07% — small in isolation, but they're the cheapest expected-value upgrade available without counting.
