S17 Notes

A,7 vs Ace is Stand under S17 — under H17 (most US Strip games) it flips to Hit. Same for A,7 vs 2: Stand here.

A,8 vs 6 stays Stand under S17. Under H17, six-deck composition flips this cell to Double.

Soft hands cannot bust on a one-card draw, which is why the doubling band is wide and aggressive against a weak dealer (4–6). When doubling is disallowed by the table or position, fall back to the second action — hit on D, stand on Ds.
